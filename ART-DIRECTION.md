# ART-DIRECTION — 2.5D isometric

## Look (v0.2)

- **Camera:** soft isometric — diamond city pads, ribbon roads, cart token  
- **Palette:** parchment `#f4e4c1`, teal `#3a8ea8`, brick `#c45c26`, seal red `#b01e2e`, gold `#d4a017`  
- **Cities:** distinct original buildings (河口 brick + flag, 青山 green roof + trees, 南浦 harbor + sailboat)  
- **Motion:** cart bob, flag/sail sway, water shimmer (CSS)  
- **UI:** paper panels, seal CTA, 繁中, NPC portrait placeholders (procedural SVG)  

## Rules

- **No** ripped sprites, maps, logos, or names from 巨商 / Gersang / Joyon  
- Code-made SVG + emoji cargo icons are correct until a commissioned tileset
