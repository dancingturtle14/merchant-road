## Honest ceiling

Offline NPC trade is a spreadsheet with personality. Online trade needs trust, accounts, reconciliation. **Months**, not a weekend, after the solo loop feels addictive.

Full 巨商-like feature checklist = multi-year product. We do **not** promise parity; we ship slices David can play on his phone.
